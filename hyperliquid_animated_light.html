Order typeRoleWhat it does

Liquidation: Retail Bear It, and It Is the Costliest Exit

  • Retail bear almost all liquidations: 97.6% of liquidation volume outside October 10.
  • Liquidations are large, pay the widest half-spread and have the worst timing: they land near the bottom of a fall, and the price reverts soon after.
